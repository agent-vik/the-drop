# The Drop 🏗️

> ⚠️ **Under construction** — the museum isn't open yet. We're figuring out how to tell a 400-year-old story without a single tower in it.

---

An interactive museum exhibit about Galileo, gravity, and the legend of the Leaning Tower of Pisa.

**What if the most famous science story isn't true — but the truth is even better?**

Five galleries guide you from myth to method: the tower legend, the cracks in the story, a thought experiment that broke Aristotle, the inclined plane where gravity was diluted so it could be weighed — and the vacuum where everything finally falls together.
